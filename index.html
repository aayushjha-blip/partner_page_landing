TCS × Rockwell & Regal Rexnord
How Tata Consultancy Services can contribute, collaborate, and build long-term partnerships
🌐 TCS at a Glance
TCS (Tata Consultancy Services) is a $30B+ revenue global IT services and digital transformation leader operating in 55 countries across 202 delivery centers. It has built a strong industrial and manufacturing practice — most recently signing an MoU with ABB (March 2026) to combine ABB's automation leadership with TCS's AI, cloud, and IT capabilities. This positions TCS as a natural strategic partner for industrial automation companies like Rockwell Automation and Regal Rexnord.
🔵 TCS × Rockwell Automation
Key collaboration and service opportunities
☁️
IT Infrastructure & Cloud Modernization
Rockwell's pivot to cloud MES (Plex) and ARR-driven model requires enterprise IT backbone. TCS can manage cloud migration, hybrid infrastructure, SAP/ERP integration, and DevOps pipelines at scale.
🤖
AI & Industrial Intelligence
TCS's AI/ML capabilities can be layered on Rockwell's FactoryTalk suite — enabling predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, and operational intelligence for Rockwell's customer base.
🔐
OT/IT Cybersecurity
With Rockwell's Verve acquisition, TCS can co-deliver OT (Operational Technology) cybersecurity services — a fast-growing need in critical infrastructure, energy, and manufacturing sectors.
🔧
Engineering & Product Services
TCS's embedded engineering practice can support Rockwell in firmware, controls software testing, hardware-software co-design, and product lifecycle management for Allen-Bradley lines.
📊
Lifecycle Services Augmentation
TCS can extend Rockwell's Lifecycle Services segment with managed services, remote NOC/SOC support, 24×7 helpdesk, and field engineering—driving margin expansion.
🌏
Global Delivery & Cost Optimization
Rockwell's global expansion in EMEA/APAC can be supported by TCS's low-cost delivery centers in India, Southeast Asia, and Eastern Europe for IT ops and support functions.
🟡 TCS × Regal Rexnord
Key collaboration and service opportunities
🏭
Manufacturing Operations Transformation
Regal Rexnord's 80+ global facilities benefit from TCS's smart manufacturing frameworks — integrating MES, SCADA, IoT sensors, and real-time dashboards across 20 decentralized divisions.
⚙️
ERP & Supply Chain Digitization
With a decentralized 20-division model, Regal Rexnord needs unified ERP/SCM visibility. TCS's SAP & Oracle practice can standardize operations and improve planning efficiency.
📈
Data & Analytics for AMC Segment
Regal's Automation & Motion Control segment is growing fastest. TCS can build analytics platforms tracking motor performance, condition data, and predictive failure signals for B2B customers.
🌱
Sustainability & ESG Reporting
Regal Rexnord's published Sustainability Report and energy-efficient mandate aligns with TCS's sustainability practice — enabling carbon tracking, ESG compliance, and green supply chain advisory.
🔄
Post-M&A Integration Support
Regal Rexnord is the product of multiple large mergers (Altra, Rexnord PMC). TCS's integration practice can harmonize IT systems, HR platforms, and customer data across legacy entities.
💼
Talent & Managed Workforce
TCS's managed staffing model can support Regal Rexnord's lean HQ with augmented engineering talent, project management, and back-office outsourcing across global facilities.
